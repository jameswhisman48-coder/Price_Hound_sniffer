# PriceHound — Google Play listing package

## Store listing
- **App name:** PriceHound
- **Short description (≤80 chars):** Compare grocery prices and find weekly deals near you by zip code.
- **Full description:**
  PriceHound helps you sniff out lower grocery prices and local deals by zip code.

  Try the zip-code demo to see how a neighborhood price comparison can work. Our Cincinnati pilot includes real Kroger online pickup prices fetched from Kroger's official API, with timestamps and a note that online prices may differ in-store. The demo also shows clearly labeled sample data while we expand coverage.

  PriceHound also links you to retailers' own published weekly-ad pages, so you can check current offers at Kroger, Aldi, Target, Walmart, and Meijer without PriceHound republishing those ads. A small pet-deals pilot links to participating retailers' sale pages too.

  PriceHound is free to explore. The optional PriceHound Plus offer is currently a one-time $3.99 purchase for the ad-free tier as it exists today. Manual renewal applies; automatic recurring billing and additional membership features such as grocery lists, automatic ordering, and delivery scheduling are roadmap items, not promises of the current app.

  Prices, availability, locations, and retailer terms can change. Always confirm details with the retailer before purchasing.

## Data safety — draft answers
- **Does the app collect or share user data?** Collects: Yes. Shares with third parties: payment information is entered into Stripe-hosted checkout and handled by Stripe; PriceHound does not receive card numbers. Confirm final Play disclosures against the exact Console prompts and Stripe integration.
- **Data types collected:** email address (waitlist; app functionality/communication), approximate location inferred from manually entered zip code (app functionality/analytics), and app interactions/analytics events (analytics). Anonymous visitor ID is stored in browser local storage and sent with events.
- **Data types not collected by PriceHound:** precise device location, contacts, photos, audio, files, phone numbers, passwords, card numbers, IP addresses, user agents, and health/financial data by PriceHound.
- **Purpose:** app functionality, analytics, and communication/waitlist.
- **Is data encrypted in transit?** Yes, the published site and Stripe checkout use HTTPS.
- **Can users request deletion?** Yes for waitlist information; contact the team via the site. Users can clear local storage to remove the anonymous browser ID.
- **Is data sold?** No.
- **Does the app collect data ephemerally?** No for the server-stored waitlist and analytics records.
