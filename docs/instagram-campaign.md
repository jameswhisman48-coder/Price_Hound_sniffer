# PriceHound — Instagram Campaign Strategy
(Research by agent-researcher, 2026-08-02. Hashtag sizes NOT live-verified; rotate sets per post.)

## 1. Paste-ready hashtag sets
- **Broad grocery savings/deals:** #grocerydeals #groceryshopping #grocerysavings #groceryhaul #budgetshopping #savemoney #moneysavingtips #couponing #coupons #dealhunting #weeklyad #frugalliving
- **Builder-story / startup:** #buildinpublic #indieapp #indiehacker #startupjourney #founderjourney #buildsomething #solopreneur #bootstrappedstartup #earlystagestartup #productlaunch #betalaunch
- **Cincinnati pilot / nationwide angle:** #cincinnati #cincinnatiohio #cincy #cincinnatideals #cincinnatishopping #ohiodeals #ohiobusiness #nky #grocerydeals #grocerysavings #groceryshopping #pricecomparison
  - (Local tags plausible but not live-verified. Never imply nationwide coverage — "starting with Cincinnati, building toward more locations.")

## 2. Five honest post concepts
1. "What does milk cost near Cincinnati today?" — Kroger-price screenshot with date + "online pickup price; may differ in-store." CTA: See the Cincinnati pilot — link in bio.
2. "I'm building a grocery price app from one ZIP code." — founder photo/text card; early stage, why Cincinnati. CTA: Join the beta list.
3. "A grocery deal is only useful if shoppers can actually find it." — screen recording of ZIP demo + weekly-ad link-out. CTA: Try your ZIP and tell us what you want next.
4. "Ten real Kroger prices, one quick comparison." — carousel of 3-5 products with fetch date + provenance. CTA: Explore the live prices.
5. "Pet deals are our small demand test." — PetSmart link-out, clearly labeled pilot (not PriceHound inventory). CTA: Check the deals and join the beta.

## 3. Cadence & mechanics
- 3-4 feed posts/week: at least 2 Reels + 1 useful carousel/static.
- Reels = stronger non-follower discovery; carousels = saves/shares.
- Stories on posting days with link sticker; one clear site URL in bio (tagged URL so IG traffic is separable in analytics).
- Reply to every comment; ask specific Cincinnati questions; reshare to Stories; 10-15 min/day engaging local grocery/frugal/neighborhood accounts.

## 4. Realistic expectations
- New account = little organic reach even with good hashtags. Hashtags categorize/search, don't guarantee distribution.
- Early growth: friends' shares > community groups > beta invites > cross-posting (TikTok/Threads/Facebook/LinkedIn/Reddit) > useful price posts shared by users.

## 5. First 2-3 week metrics (from site analytics)
- PRIMARY: unique visitors, ZIP-demo completions, beta + waitlist signups.
- Best signal: visitor enters ZIP then joins a list.
- SECONDARY: pilot weekly-ad clicks (useful intent); pet link clicks (separate demand test).
- Compare by post format/CTA, not likes. Plus purchases too sparse initially to be a KPI.
