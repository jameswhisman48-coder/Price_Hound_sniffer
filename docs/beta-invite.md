# PriceHound beta invite

## Friends-and-family pilot

PriceHound is inviting a small group of friends and family to try the Cincinnati, Ohio (45202) pilot. Test the zip-code experience, compare the live Kroger online pickup prices, and follow the retailer weekly-ad links.

### What to test

- Enter your ZIP code and confirm the results are clearly labeled as demo, pilot, or retailer-sourced data.
- Check whether the prices, timestamps, and “may differ in-store” provenance note are understandable.
- Open a weekly-ad or pet-deals link and confirm it takes you to the retailer’s own site.
- Report confusing copy, broken links, mobile layout issues, or inaccurate expectations.

### Important limits

This is an early pilot, not a guarantee of savings or product availability. Retailer prices and terms can change; confirm details with the retailer before buying. Please do not submit sensitive personal information or share private beta feedback publicly without permission.

Send feedback through the invitation channel you received. Include your device, browser, ZIP code (if relevant), and the page or action where you noticed an issue.
